Privacy Policy for Effzaly

Introduction

Effzaly is a Chrome extension designed to translate Arabic text and speech into sign language. This privacy policy explains how Effzaly collects, uses, and protects user data to ensure transparency and compliance with applicable laws.

Data We Collect

  • Website Content: The extension processes text selected by the user on webpages to generate sign language translations. No other website content (e.g., images, sounds, or videos) is collected.

Note: Effzaly does not collect any personally identifiable information (e.g., name, email, or address), financial data, health data, or authentication credentials.

How We Use Your Data

The data is only used to provide the core functionality of the extension:

  • Translating selected text into sign language videos.
  • Playing corresponding sign language videos for educational and accessibility purposes.

Data Storage

Effzaly does not store any user data. All processing is done locally on the user’s device. Temporary data, such as selected text, is cleared after use and is not saved on any servers.

Data Sharing

Effzaly does not sell, share, or transfer user data to third parties. All processing is confined to the user’s device to ensure privacy and security.

Permissions

The following permissions are required for Effzaly to function:

  • Website Content: To process user-selected text for translation.
  • Active Tab: To access and interact with the content of the current webpage.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically to reflect changes to our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Users are encouraged to review the privacy policy regularly for updates.
